What the platform promises

Each promise is built into the software, not just written in a form.

Your file stays on your device

The export is opened in your browser. Parts the study never collects are dropped before you even see them.

You see exactly what is sent

The list you review is the same data that is uploaded. The server double-checks that nothing changed on the way.

Study rules are locked

What a study may collect, how long it keeps data and how it deletes it are published once. Changing them means asking you again.

Studies are walled off

One study can never see another study's participants or data, even on the same website.

No names, no emails

You take part through a private link and a code. If you give an email for reminders, it is kept apart and never becomes research data.

Deleting takes two people

Withdrawing cuts off access at once. Erasing the data needs one staff member to propose it and a different one to approve it.

Three layers, each one only narrower

The software can only read certain things. Each study may allow only some of those. You may share only some of what the study allows. Pick a service to see it.

Ready

Only services the team has checked accept uploads. Some show instructions only. This study uses three of them.

Who it is for

Participants

  • Plain steps, one decision at a time
  • Never asked for a name
  • See counts first; details only if you want
  • Withdraw any time; earned payment stays

Research teams

  • Run several studies in one place
  • Set services, rounds and consent per study
  • See progress without seeing raw data
  • Every download is logged

Ethics boards

  • Every promise to participants is published and locked
  • An incomplete setup cannot go live
  • Data is kept and deleted on schedule
  • A full record of who did what, per study

    5 · Where data goes

    Three places, one direction

    The same rules run on your device and on the research server, so nothing slips through in either place. Your raw file has no route to the server at all.

    Three rules that never bend

    Studies are walled off

    • Every record is tagged with its study. The database refuses links between studies.
    • A private link only works for the study it was made for.
    • A wrong link and an expired link get the same plain error, so nothing leaks.

    Rules cannot change quietly

    • What a study collects, how long it keeps data, how it deletes it and how it pays are published together as one locked version.
    • Every request re-checks that the version is still the same one.
    • Changing any of those terms creates a new version and asks participants again.

    A code, not a name

    • Participants join through a private link. No account, no password.
    • Sessions are short-lived and checked on every request.
    • An optional email for reminders is stored apart and never becomes research data.

    Every date comes from the study's published rules.
